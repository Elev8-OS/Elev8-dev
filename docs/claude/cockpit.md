### Cockpit (`/cockpit`, `app/components/cockpit/`)

Every listing on one tape chart, styled after a hotel booking chart the user picked: slanted bars, a header row per listing with free units and a price pill, a date header with arrivals and occupancy. The old owner calendar card (`PortalReservationCalendar`) was removed from this page; that component still lives in the owner portal (`owner-portal/stays.vue`).

#### Data: Reservations module only
- ⚠️ Stays come from **`useReservationsModule` only** (what the Reservations page lists), never `listing.bookings`, so every bar is a reservation and opens `ReservationDetailSheet` (looked up live by id). They are drawn through `reservationToBooking`, which keeps the reservation id (owner requests and maintenance blocks become blocks).
- `data/cockpit.ts` is framework-free; specs in `tests/lib/cockpit.spec.ts`.
  - `buildCockpitGroups(listings, reservations)`: one group per active listing, by name. A single-unit listing has one `unit` row (id = listing id); a multi-unit listing has one `room` row per active room (`<listingId>:<unitId>`, stays by `stayUnitIds`) plus `Not assigned to a room` when a stay has no room.
  - Nights: `occupiesNight` counts the arrival night, not the departure. Cancelled stays and inquiries hold nothing (`isLiveStay`). A listing `blockedDates` entry closes every unit. `freeUnits` counts an unassigned stay against the free rooms.
  - `nightlyRateOn` (seasonal rate covering the night, else `pricing.nightlyRate`), `listingCurrency`, `formatMoney` (`ISO amount`).
  - `availableRowIds` (Find availability), `filterGroups` (name search + tags, AND).
  - `conflictIds`: two live stays (not cancelled, not inquiry) sharing a night in one unit or room row. The unassigned row is never a conflict.
  - `periodSummary(groups, firstKey, lastKey, todayKey, actionNeeded)`: booking ids for the period on the board: arrivals and departures on a day shown, staying (a night shown), no room yet / double booked (a night shown, not over yet), action needed (a night or the departure shown, not over yet). Blocks are not guests. ⚠️ It follows the period by the user's choice; it was today-only (`todaySummary`) before.
  - `stayVisible` / `CockpitStayFilters` (statuses, channels, `showCancelled`, `actionOnly`): filters hide BARS only; availability always counts every stay. `groupsWithAction`, `hasStaysIn`, `activeFilterCount`.
  - `portfolioOccupancy`, `arrivalsOn` (each reservation once, though a multi-room stay sits on several rows), `nowFraction`, `selectionStay`.
  - Ranges: `COCKPIT_RANGES` (1 / 2 / 4 weeks, 1 Month, Custom), `rangeLength`, `addIsoMonths` (clamps to month end), `MAX_CUSTOM_DAYS` = 92.
- `data/cockpit-prefs.ts`: period, custom length, tags, filters and folded listings, per browser in `localStorage` (`elev8.cockpit.prefs`), guarded on `typeof localStorage` and try/catch; missing fields fall back. Loaded after mount (SSR-safe), saved on change. The start date is not kept: the board opens on today (on 1 Month, the 1st of this month). Specs in `tests/lib/cockpit-prefs.spec.ts`.

#### Page (`app/pages/cockpit.vue`)
- Toolbar: the period as the title ("October to November 2026", `h1` with a screen-reader "Cockpit, " prefix) over the exact dates, then « (one month back) ‹ (one period back) Today › », centred (see Page header). « » are hidden on 1 Month, where ‹ › already step a month. 1 Month always starts on the 1st (`monthStart`; switching to it, Today and Find availability all snap there) and steps by calendar month.
- `CockpitTodayStrip`: counts from `periodSummary`, with no visible heading (the page title already names the period; `periodLabel` is only in the `aria-label`), so moving the month changes them; a past period shows nothing to act on. A count focuses its stays (others fade, `focusIds`), again clears; the stays are in the period, so focusing never moves the board. Owner requests (all pending, not by period) open the approvals sheet (`OwnerStayApprovalsPanel`).
- Empty-period note when no reservation touches the period, linking to Reservations.
- Action needed: the page builds the reservation id set from `useInbox` conversations with `status: 'action_needed'` and passes it down, so the bar never imports the Inbox store. The marker opens the conversation via `inbox.openForReservation` then `/inbox`, like the sheet's Inbox button.
- Click or drag on a unit row (`selectionStay`): the date dragged FROM is the check-in and the date dragged TO is the check-out, as the bar is drawn midday to midday, so dragging the 8th to the 9th is one night, 8 to 9. A click on one date is one night from it. ⚠️ An earlier rule read every touched date as a night (8 and 9 became 8 to 10); the user read that as wrong. While dragging, a slanted preview bar shows where the stay will sit and its night count (red when a night is taken). Then `CockpitCreateDialog`, a right-side **Sheet** (not a modal, by the user's choice; room name, listing, the dates and nights, and a gear button beside the close X that switches the sheet to override settings, see below), offers New reservation (opens `NewReservationDialog` with `prefill` listing, dates and the row's `unitId`; on `created`, a room row's room is assigned with `assignRoom`) or Block nights (`createReservation` with `status: 'blocked'`, the reason in `guestNotes`, then `assignRoom`). A range with a taken night is refused with a toast.
- **Double booking check on create** (`newReservationConflicts` in `useReservationsModule.ts`, spec `tests/composables/new-reservation-conflicts.spec.ts`): `NewReservationDialog` runs it on Create Reservation, from any page. Any clash opens an AlertDialog listing the stays (guest or "Blocked nights", dates, shared rooms, status) with Go back / Create anyway. Rules: cancelled stays and inquiries with `blocksAvailability: false` never count, blocks do; a single-unit listing clashes on any shared night; on a multi-unit listing a stay clashes when it holds a room the new booking names (priced room lines, else the Cockpit row's `prefill.unitId`), or when the listing runs out of rooms on a shared night (a stay without a room takes one). A new inquiry that does not hold the dates skips the check.
- **Date overrides** (`data/cockpit-overrides.ts`, store `useCockpitOverrides` = the only writer, specs `tests/lib/cockpit-overrides.spec.ts`): the create sheet's gear opens "Override settings" for the picked nights (check-in to the night before check-out). Settings, in the user's order: Only Availability, Closed To Arrival, Closed To Departure, Max Availability, Max Stay, Min Stay, Rate, Stop Sell (`OVERRIDE_FIELDS`); tick one to edit it (custom checkbox rows, `Switch` for on/off, counts capped at the type's rooms, Rate with the ISO code prefix), `validateOverride` before saving, toast on save. Overrides already on those nights are listed with Remove. ⚠️ Scope is the **room type** of the row clicked (every room of it) or the whole property for a single-unit listing, never one room and never one rate plan (Channex keeps availability per room type); a later override wins field by field (`overridesOn`). Mock: in-memory `useState`, nothing goes to a channel, and booking and availability rules do not read them yet. The free counts on the listing and room type header rows show rooms **open to sell** (`sellableUnits`: Stop Sell 0, Only Availability at most that many, Max Availability caps rooms sold, taken rooms counting against it; never more than really free), underlined in primary when an override lowers them, the tooltip saying "1 of 3 rooms open to sell (2 free)"; a multi-unit listing's header takes off what each type's overrides close. Room rows still show bookable free nights (overrides do not stop a booking yet). On the board: an override rate replaces the rate pill (primary outline) on the listing or room type header row, any other overridden setting adds a sliders icon beside the free count, and the band tooltip lists them. The sheet only opens on free nights (a range with a taken night is refused), so overrides cannot be set from booked nights.
- **Change history** (`useCockpitLog`, sheet `CockpitHistorySheet.vue`, spec `tests/composables/useCockpitLog.spec.ts`): the header's **History** button (beside New reservation) opens a right sheet of what users changed from the board, newest first, grouped by local day (Today, Yesterday, then the date), with filter chips and counts (All, Reservations, Blocks, Rooms, Overrides). Each entry: icon, title, a sentence (guest or room, dates, what changed), the user (`useCurrentDashboardUser`, so it reads Komang Juliantara in the demo) and time, and the listing. One with a reservation opens it (`ReservationDetailSheet`). Logged: a reservation created from the page's New reservation (with its room), nights blocked (with the reason), a stay put in / moved between / taken out of a room, an override saved or removed (scope, nights, settings). Not logged: changes made inside other sheets the board opens (reservation, cleaning, task), which keep their own activity. Mock: in-memory `useState`, empty on a fresh session.
- Drag a bar onto a room of its listing (or back onto Not assigned): `assignRoom` / `unassignRoom`, placement only. Only stays without priced room lines can move (`canAssignRoom`).

- **Today button** (`goToToday`): moves to the period holding today, then calls the board's exposed `scrollToToday()` so today's column is the first one after the listing column (smooth, instant under reduced motion). The board also scrolls there once when it mounts.
- **Loading**: the board is `<ClientOnly>`; its fallback is `CockpitBoardSkeleton.vue`, the same frame (listing column, date header, three listings with bars) in `Skeleton` blocks, fixed shapes so SSR and client agree, `role="status"` with a screen-reader "Loading the board".
- Page header: title left, Find availability / History / New reservation right (never wrapping among themselves from `md`), the date controls (« ‹ Today › », period select, custom range picker) centred between them on ONE line, but only when that really fits: `headerOneLine` measures the three groups with `useResizeObserver` (2 x the wider side group + the date controls + 2 gaps <= the header width). Otherwise the date controls take a second line under the title, **aligned left** with the strip below. On a phone everything stacks. ⚠️ History of what the user rejected: a viewport `2xl` breakpoint (broke with the sidebar open and the custom range), container-query guesses, and a lone centred second line ("jelek"). Checked headless at 2000, 1700 and 1300px with Custom and 1 Month.

#### Board (`CockpitTimeline.vue`)
- ⚠️ **Rates are per room type.** A multi-unit listing's header row shows only its total free rooms; under it, each room type (`CockpitGroup.types`, `CockpitRoomType`) has its own header row with that type's free rooms (`typeFreeUnits`; a stay with no room counts against no type) and its own rate pill (`roomTypeRate`: the type's base rate plan, else its first, else the listing's `pricing.nightlyRate`; in the type's currency), then its rooms. Room types fold on their own (`<listingId>:type:<typeId>` in the folded list). "Not assigned to a room" comes last. A single-unit listing has no room types (the seed data gives none), so its header shows the listing rate (`nightlyRateOn`, with seasonal rates). Seasonal rates live on the listing only, so a room type's pill does not change by season.
- One CSS grid per row: `var(--cockpit-left)` (`9rem` phone, `17rem` from `md`) then two half-day columns per day. Every listing has a header row (name, rooms and location, fold chevron; per day the free units, red at 0, and for a single-unit listing a `USD 185` rate pill, whole amounts, full rate in the tooltip) over its unit rows, which hold only bars. A single-unit listing's unit row is named by `listing.room`, else "Whole property".
- Date header: arrivals badge (hidden at 0), weekday, large date, occupancy %. Month name on the first day and every 1st, with a stronger line. Today's column is tinted `primary/10` across the board, today's date is a gold pill, and a gold line with a time pill marks now (`nowFraction`, ticks each minute). ⚠️ The pill hangs half below the sticky header (its own stacking context), so the listing column cannot cover that half: `updateNowPill` hides it (`invisible`) once its left edge slides under the header's left cell, on scroll, resize and when the line moves.
- Bars stack in lanes (`assignStayLanes`). Cancelled stays get their own lanes below the live ones: each is a `1.5rem` grid row whose bar aligns to the bottom and overflows up, so it tucks under the bar above; earlier lanes paint over later ones.
- Gold dots mark a bar's end that arrives or leaves today.
- **Listing score** (`CockpitListingScore.vue`): a ring gauge beside each listing's name on its header row, the Listing Setup completion (`listingSetupProgress().percent`, same number as the setup page). Red under 50, amber under 80, primary from 80. Hover shows each section's done/total and a "Still missing" list from `section.missing` (field labels); a click opens Listing Setup.
- **Channel links** (`CockpitChannelLinks.vue`): ⚠️ channels map **room types**, so a multi-unit listing shows the logos on each **room type** header row (after "N rooms"), never on its listing row; a single-unit listing (no room types) shows them on its header row after the location. One small OTA logo per channel the listing has a mapping on (`Listing.otaConnected` + `useChannels().getMapping`), with the sync status dot as in the Listings table (green live, amber syncing, red error). A logo opens that page on the OTA in a new tab (`channelListingUrl` in `channels/data/channels.ts`: Airbnb and Vrbo by the OTA id, hotel-style channels such as Booking.com by the account's `propertyId`); a channel with no public page (Google) shows the logo only. A room type's OTA id is `roomTypeExternalId(listingId, typeId, channel)` (mock, stable), since `useChannels` still keeps one mapping per listing; status and account come from that listing mapping. The tooltip names the room type, status, OTA id (channel's `listingIdLabel`), account and any sync error. Read only, never writes channel data.
- **Room tasks** (`CockpitRoomTasks.vue`): every unit or room row (not Not assigned) shows its open task count as a round badge, grey at 0 and red from 1. A click opens a popover listing the room's open tasks (overdue and soonest due first, then priority) with status, priority and due date; picking one opens `TaskDetailSheet` via `useTaskDetail`. The page builds `openTasks` (row id to `Task[]`): a single-unit listing's row takes every open task of the listing, a room row the tasks naming its `unitId`; a multi-unit task with no room is on no row.
- **Room cleaning status** (`data/cockpit-cleaning.ts`, `roomCleaningStatus`, specs `tests/lib/cockpit-cleaning.spec.ts`): each unit or room row shows today's housekeeping state as a round icon in the left column: ⚠️ always the same broom (`ROOM_CLEANING_ICON`), only the colour changes, by the user's choice: red Dirty, green Clean, blue Cleaning, grey Occupied (`roomCleaningMeta`). No word on the board; the state and full sentence are in the tooltip and `aria-label`. Rules, in order: a cleaning `in_progress` today is **Cleaning**; a guest who arrived before today and has not left is **Occupied**; a guest departure (today or earlier) with no `done` cleaning since is **Dirty** (a past cleaning that never started reads as missed, as on the Operations Calendar); else **Clean**. Jobs come from `useCleaningJobs`; a room row takes jobs naming its `unitId`, a single-unit row every job of the listing. Blocks and cancelled stays never dirty a room. Not assigned to a room has no chip. The page builds the map (`roomCleaning`) and passes it to the board. **The icon is a button**: `roomCleaningStatus` also returns `jobId`, the cleaning it rests on (in progress; occupied or dirty: the next one, else for dirty the missed one; clean: the last one done, else the next). A click emits `openCleaning`; the page opens the Operations Calendar cleaning sheet (`CalendarEventDetailDialog`, event from `buildCleaningEvents(undefined, [job])`) for it, or, with no job, `OperationsCalendarCreateDialog only="cleaning"` for that listing and room, dated today.
- Corner tools (`CockpitBoardTools.vue`, above the board on a phone): colour legend and fold all on the first line, then search with the Filters popover beside it (Action needed only, Show cancelled, Status, Channel, Tags; `ScrollArea` needs a fixed height). New reservation sits in the page header beside Find availability.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move between bars (left/right in the row, up/down to the nearest bar by date); Enter opens.
- ⚠️ History of what users rejected, do not bring back: rates printed only where they change (read as missing info); values only in the afternoon half (empty mornings); a tinted occupancy band without numbers.

#### Bar (`CockpitStayBar.vue`)
- One line (`h-8`), slanted ends via `clip-path` where the stay really starts or ends, square where it runs off the view. ⚠️ **Status colours are `stayBarClasses`, unchanged** (the user asked to keep them). Stays already checked out are lighter; cancelled are grey and hatched. On the bar: the booking channel logo (`otaIcon(booking.source)`, globe for direct) on a small `bg-background` round chip so it reads on every status colour, then the name; blocks have no chip. After the name (not on blocks): the check-in time (`getCheckInTime`, else the listing default; clock icon), the guest count (adults + children + infants; users icon) and, when the stay has upsell orders, a bag on a `bg-background` chip. The page builds `upsellCounts` (reservation id to orders not declined, matched by `order.reservationId` or the stay's legacy `upsellIds`, as in `ReservationDetailSheet`). They give way as the bar narrows, by container query on the bar button: time from `@min-[14rem]` (about two nights), guests from `@min-[10rem]`, bag from `@min-[6rem]`; the name is never dropped. Everything is also in the bar's `aria-label`. Check-out time and the guest breakdown stay in `ListingStayHoverCard`, which closes on any scroll (capture listener) so it never floats away from its bar.
- Because of `clip-path`, outer rings are clipped: the conflict mark and the focus ring are `ring-inset`; a conflict also shows an alert icon.
- **Extensions** (`extensionJoins`): an extension is its own reservation with `extensionOf` = the original's id. Where it meets the part of the same stay it touches (the original, or an earlier extension, so chains link too; nights added after meet on that part's check-out, an early arrival on its check-in), the board draws a round link icon in the slanted gap, on the stay's lane. Clicking it opens the extension. Only when both bars are drawn on that row and the date is in view; a guest who merely arrives as another leaves never gets one. ⚠️ **A direct stay extended in place is drawn split, like an OTA stay** (the user asked: not one joined bar): `barSegments` cuts its bar at midday of each in-place join date (`splitSpan`), so the stay and each extension are separate bars meeting slant to slant, same booking, same lane; the Action needed dot only on the first part. Lanes, keyboard moves, today marks and the counts still use the whole stay. It also gets the link icon (the user asked for it): it keeps one reservation and one bar, so `inPlaceExtensionJoins` reads its `extensions` (not cancelled, no `linkedReservationId`) and draws the icon inside the bar at the old check-out (or old check-in for an early arrival), only while that date is still inside the bar. Tooltip: "Guest: extended, 2 nights added from 15 Oct. Open the stay."; a click opens the stay.
- The Action needed marker is a red dot at the bar's start, a sibling button (not inside the bar button).
- `ListingStayHoverCard` takes an optional `v-model:open`; the bar closes it on click so it never covers the sheet.
- Cockpit-only: the listing calendar keeps the one-line `ListingStayBar`.
