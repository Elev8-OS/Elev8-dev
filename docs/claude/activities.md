# Activities (`/activities`)

> Time and activity report per staff member. Structure follows the v2 dashboard (staff list, then a per-person detail), rebuilt on the new design system. Mock/demo: logs are generated, nothing is sent anywhere.

## Pages
- `app/pages/activities/index.vue`: one summary card (staff with activity, total activity and total time each with a change vs the previous period, time by type), a toolbar (search staff, role, listing, clear), and a sortable table. Columns: Staff, Role, Activity, Per day (sparkline), Time (bar against the target plus %), Flags. Below `xl` the flags collapse into "N to check" under the name; below `md` Role hides, below `lg` the sparkline hides. Export to .xlsx, and a pager (staff).
- `app/pages/activities/[id].vue`: person header with the range bar, **Listings**, **History** (change count) and **Add activity**; a summary card (total time with % of target, total activity, "Needs a look", time by type); a **Time per day** bar chart (dashed line = the person's `hoursPerDay`); search plus type toggles; logs grouped by day (newest first), paged by **day** (5, 10 or 20 per page).

## Data and state
- Types, seed generator and helpers: `app/components/activities/data/activities.ts`. A log is `{ userId, listingId, type: 'cleaning' | 'task' | 'tracker', title, date, durationMinutes, notes?, manual?, editedAt?, editedBy? }`.
- **Log types**: `cleaning` (a cleaning job, housekeeping roles only, **filed under a kind of cleaning**), `task` (a task), and `tracker` (**Activity tracker**: free time a person tracks themselves, not tied to a job or task; the mobile app's `activity_tracking` module). The id used to be `hourly` in earlier drafts, which was a guess from the v2 icon name.
- **Cleaning logs are by cleaning type**: `ActivityLog.cleaningType` is one of the Operations Calendar's `CleaningType` (`daily`, `check_out`, `mid_stay`, `custom`, `vacant`; `CLEANING_LOG_TYPES`), and the title is that type's label (`cleaningLogTitle`: "Daily cleaning", "Check-out cleaning", "Mid-stay cleaning", "Custom cleaning", "Vacant upkeep"), so cleanings line up with the cleaning checklists. There is no typed title for a cleaning: Add activity swaps "What was done" for a **Cleaning type** select when Type is Cleaning, and `addLog` refuses a cleaning without a kind and names it from the kind (a `title` passed for a cleaning is ignored). Folded rows and "Longer than usual" therefore group cleanings by kind. Seed durations follow the kind (`CLEANING_MINUTES`: a check-out 90 to 180 min, a daily 25 to 60). A task or tracker log never has a `cleaningType`. **Filter by kind** (detail page): while the Cleaning type toggle is on, a "Cleaning type" row of chips appears (Daily, Check-out, Mid-stay, Custom, Vacant upkeep) with each kind's count in the range and scope (`cleaningKindCounts`); only kinds the person has logs for show, plus any picked one. `dayGroups(..., { cleaningTypes })` narrows **cleaning logs only**, so a task or tracker log that is also switched on stays; no chips picked means no narrowing, and an old cleaning with no kind is hidden once one is picked. Turning Cleaning off clears the chips, and Clear filters clears them too
- Seed logs cover the last 90 days **relative to today**, deterministic (seeded PRNG), limited to each user's `listingIds`, 3 to 6 logs a working day. Sundays and about one weekday in eight are skipped. The inactive Pool user has nothing in the last 28 days.
- `useActivityReport()` (`useState('activity-logs')`, `'activity-audit'`, `'activity-range'`): `staffRows(range, filters)`, `totals(range, filters)`, `dayGroups(userId, range, { types, query, includeOtherTenants })`, `dailySeries`, `listingSummary`, `byTypeFor`, `otherTenantCount`, `tenantsFor`, `listingsFor`, `targetMinutes`, `isLong`, `addLog`, `updateLog`, `removeLog`, `auditFor`. Mutations use spread/`map`/`filter`, never in-place edits.
- Pure helpers (`app/lib/activity-stats.ts`): `eachDay`, `isWorkDay`, `workDaysIn`, `previousRange`, `median`, `deltaPercent`, `pageWindow`. Range presets: Today, Last 7 days (default), Last 2 weeks, This month, Last month, Custom. The chosen range is shared by list and detail.

## Rules and gotchas
- ⚠️ Dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD` keys (`toLocalDateKey`), never `toISOString()` (shifts a UTC+8 midnight). The picker converts to and from `CalendarDate`.
- **Target** = the user's `hoursPerDay` times working days in the range, Monday to Saturday (Sunday is the day off), never counting days after today. With a **listing filter** on there is no target (0), because the time is only part of the day: the bar and % hide.
- **Who is listed**: anyone with logs in the range, plus active people who have ever logged, so a quiet week shows them (0 activity, "days not logged"). Inactive people with nothing in the range are left out. With a listing filter only people with logs at that listing remain.
- **Days not logged** = working days up to yesterday with no log (today is left out, the day is not over). **Longer than usual** = a log over 1.5x the median of the same type and title, at least 15 min over, needing 5 samples of that work. Both are hints, not rules.
- **Folded rows**: logs of the same type, title and listing on the same day show as one row with `×N` and the summed time; expanding shows each log with its own actions.
- **Manager changes** (`addLog`, `updateLog`, `removeLog`) write an `ActivityAuditEntry` (who, when, what) that survives deleting the log, shown in the **History** sheet. Editing only changes duration and note (type, listing, day stay as logged). A log shows "Added by manager" (added) or "Edited" (changed, with who and when in the tooltip). An edit that changes nothing writes nothing. The actor is `useCurrentDashboardUser()`.
- **Tenants** (`Include transferred tenants` toggle, detail page): a person with several `assignedTenantIds` (today only the GRO, Komang) has logs spread over those tenants (`ActivityLog.tenantId`); everyone else has no `tenantId` and always counts. **Off (default)**: only the active tenant counts, the one picked in the sidebar (`useState('active-tenant-id')`, same default as the tenant switcher), in the summary, chart, type split, day list and the Listings sheet; a note says how many activities in other tenants are hidden. **On**: all assigned tenants count, and logs from a tenant other than the active one carry a tenant badge. `ActivityTenantBadge` ("⇄ N tenants", tooltip lists the tenants and marks the active one) sits beside the name in the list and in the detail header, and renders nothing for people with one tenant. The toggle only shows for people with more than one tenant, resets when you open another person, and is local to the page: `/activities` (the list) always counts the active tenant only, via `filters.includeOtherTenants` (unset). Folded rows never merge logs of different tenants. The target and "% of target" hide while only one of several tenants is counted (part of a day). A manager-added log for a multi-tenant person is filed under the active tenant. ⚠️ No module outside the Activities pages reads `active-tenant-id` yet, and logs are mock data spread by a separate seeded generator so the other seed values stay the same
- **Cleaning is only for housekeeping roles** (`role-housekeeping`, `role-housekeeping-manager`; `CLEANING_ROLE_IDS` and `loggableTypes(roleId)` in `activities.ts`). Everyone else logs Task and Activity tracker. Add activity offers only the types the person's role allows and `addLog` refuses a disallowed type. The detail page's type toggles and "Time by type" show `shownTypesFor(userId)`: the role's types plus any type the person already has logs for, so history never disappears if a role changes. The list's "Time by type" drops Cleaning when nobody listed can clean (for example a role filter on listing managers). The seed already only gives cleaning to the two housekeeping users
- **Listing dropdowns** use the shared `operations-calendar/ListingPicker.vue` (search, Tags, and each multi-unit listing's rooms under it via `pick-rooms`). The list's listing filter takes `v-model` (listing id, `''` = all) and `v-model:unit-id`: a listing alone filters every room of it, a room narrows to that room (`ActivityFilters.listingId` + `unitId`, `atPlace`). Add activity uses it with `restrict-to-ids` (the person's assigned listings, a prop added to the picker for this, unset elsewhere), so a room can be chosen. A log has an optional `unitId`/`unitName` (a room of a multi-unit listing; unset = the whole property), shown as `Listing · Room` (`logPlace`) in rows, dialogs, search and the history. `addLog` refuses a room that is not in the listing. Folded rows never merge logs of different rooms. The seed names a room on about two thirds of the logs at multi-unit listings, from its own generator so other values stay the same
- ⚠️ **Dialog overflow**: a shadcn `DialogContent` is a grid, so a long `nowrap` label (the picker's "Listing · Room") becomes the grid's minimum width and stretches every row past the box. The Add activity form body and the listing field carry `min-w-0`, and the dialog is `sm:max-w-xl` so the full label fits. Keep `min-w-0` on form wrappers that hold a picker
- **Storage ("the database")**: there is no backend in this demo, so the report is saved to `localStorage` (`elev8-activity-report`, `app/lib/activity-store.ts`, version-checked, guarded on `typeof localStorage`): the logs, the change history and `throughDay` (the latest day the demo data was made for). Every `addLog` / `updateLog` / `removeLog` saves it (via `record`). On a **new day** `growActivityReport` adds the demo logs for the days since `throughDay` (ids suffixed with the day) and never touches what is saved, so a manager's additions, edits and deletions are never overwritten and "Last 7 days" never runs dry. ⚠️ **Read the saved copy after mount, not in the `useState` initialiser**: Nuxt SSR sends the server's state to the browser and does not re-run initialisers, so a read there is ignored after a reload and the next save would overwrite the saved copy with the server's demo data. `useActivityReport` therefore starts from the demo report (same on server and browser, so hydration matches) and calls `restore()` in `onMounted` (immediately when there is no component, as in unit tests); nothing is written until `restored` is true. Specs reset localStorage in `beforeEach`
- **One source for the user's profile**: `/users/<id>` (Activities tab, count and last activity) reads time-tracked work from this report through `useUserActivities` (`trackedLogToActivity`: type, title, notes, `Listing · Room`, minutes, `source: 'activities'`, `dateOnly: true`, so no clock time is invented). The profile's own feed keeps only events that are not tracked work (guest messages, sign-ins, check-ins, maintenance); its old seeded cleaning and task rows were removed so nothing is counted twice, and its seed and its "30 days" count are anchored to today. The profile feed is paged (10, 25 or 50), and links to `/activities/<id>` ("Open in Activities"); the Activities detail links back ("Profile"). The Activities report is the only writer of these logs
- Add activity: only the person's assigned listings, any day from the first logged day up to today, more than 0 minutes with minutes 0 to 59.
- Pagination (`ActivityPager`): hidden while everything fits the smallest page size; page size select, numbered pages with ellipses, resets to page 1 when the range, filters or sort change.
- Durations read `20m`, `3h`, `3h 05m` (`formatDuration`).
- Export (`app/lib/activity-export.ts`, `write-excel-file` loaded on demand) writes the sorted, filtered rows as `activities-<from>_<to>.xlsx`: staff, role, activity, time, target, % of target, days not logged, longer than usual.
- ⚠️ Reka dropdown triggers (the "..." menu) ignore synthetic `pointerdown` events from scripts; open them with a real click or keyboard when checking in a browser.
- This is separate from `users/data/user-activities.ts`, the per-user event feed on the user detail page.
- Menu: Operations, after Tasks. Permission module `activity_reports` exists in the role matrix but this page is not gated yet.
- Tests: `tests/composables/useActivityReport.spec.ts`, `tests/lib/activity-stats.spec.ts`.
