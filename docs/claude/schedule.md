# Schedule (`/schedule`)

> Shifts and days off per person. The role's working hours are the default; a manager gives a person weekly days and a default shift, or changes a single date. Mock/demo: saved to `localStorage`, nothing is sent anywhere. Managers enter everything; there is no staff request flow yet.

## Where a day comes from
`resolveSchedule` (`app/lib/schedule.ts`, pure) picks, most specific first:
1. **Dated override** (`ScheduleOverride`, one per person and date): `day_off` (leave, sick, public holiday, swap, other) or `working` (an extra shift or swap: a **shift**, custom start and end, or neither, which keeps the role's hours; plus `extra` shifts, see below).
2. **The person's rotation** (`rotations[userId]`, see below), from its start date.
3. **The person's own weekly days** (`personDays[userId]`) and **default shifts** (`personShifts[userId]`, a list of shift ids, one or several). The days only decide which weekdays are worked; the hours are the default shifts', else the role's.
4. **`Role.workingHours`** (a fixed role's `shiftIds`, else its plain `startTime` and `endTime`) (`users/data/roles.ts`): `fixed` has `startTime` and `endTime`, `flexible` has none. A role listing all seven days (the flexible ones) means nobody is ever off by default, so a day off is set per person.

### Shifts
A `ShiftTemplate` is `{ id, name, start, end, breakMinutes, tone }` (Settings list on the **Shifts** sheet; demo ones are Morning 07:00 to 15:00, Day 08:00 to 17:00, Afternoon 12:00 to 20:00, Night 22:00 to 06:00). Rules (`shiftProblem`): unique names, start and end are different times, the break is shorter than the shift. **An end earlier than the start runs past midnight** (Night 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours, `shiftLength`, labelled `22:00 to 06:00 (+1)`). `tone` is one of five theme tokens (`SHIFT_TONES`), never a free colour. A resolved shift carries `name`, `tone` and `breakMinutes`, and `shiftMinutes` is its paid length (length minus break).
- ⚠️ **A shift set on a date is a snapshot.** `setWorking({ shiftId })` copies the name, tone, hours and break onto the override (`shiftId` stays as provenance), so editing or deleting the template never changes a day already set. A **default shift** is live: editing the template changes that person's coming (and shown) days, and deleting it drops it as anyone's default (`removeShift`). The Shifts sheet shows how many people and days use each.

### Several shifts in one day
A day resolves to `segments` (one to `MAX_SHIFTS_PER_DAY` = 4, in time order); `shift` is the first. Two ways to get more than one:
- **On a date**: a `working` override carries `extra` (`OverrideSegment[]`: start, end and the same name, tone, break snapshot as the first). The first needs hours (a shift or custom hours, not "hours from the role", which the dialog cannot see). `setWorking` returns the reason and saves nothing when it refuses.
- **As the weekly default**: `personShifts[userId]` holds several shift ids (the weekly dialog's "Add another shift a day"); `setPersonShifts` sorts them by start and refuses overlaps. Every working weekday then has those shifts.
- `segmentsProblem(parts)` is the one rule: different start and end, each starts **after the previous ends**, at most four. The paid minutes are the sum of the parts (the gaps are not paid), `plannedMinutesOf`.

### Rotations
`ScheduleRotation { userId, startDate (a Monday), weeks[] }` repeats by itself: week 1 is the week of `startDate`, then week 2..N (1 to `MAX_ROTATION_WEEKS` = 8), then week 1 again. A weekday in a week is `'off'`, a list of shift ids (a split shift is allowed in the data), or missing (= off). It applies from `startDate` on; days before follow the weekly days. `rotationDayFor` gives a date's cell, `resolveSchedule` takes it as the 6th argument and returns `source: 'rotation'`. A dated override still wins. One rotation per person; **Stop rotation** removes it.
- UI: the **A rotation** half of `schedule/SchedulePersonSheet.vue` (the repeat icon on a row): start date, weeks in the cycle, a Select per day (Off or a shift) and quick fills ("Mon to Fri Morning", "All off"). The editor picks **one shift per day**; a cell that already holds several keeps them untouched. Cells show a **Rotation** marker.
- `rotationProblem` refuses a cycle with a bad shift list or an overnight clash, **including the wrap** from the last week back to week 1. Deleting a shift turns the rotation days that used it into days off (`removeShift`).
- ⚠️ Editing a rotation changes every date from its start, past ones included (it is live like a default shift, not a snapshot). Nobody has a rotation in the seed. Copying a rotation to other people is not built.

### Overnight shift followed by another day
A shift past midnight keeps the next morning busy: `carryOverOf` says until when, and the grid shows **Until 06:00** with a moon on that cell. The next day's own shifts are a separate schedule, and **`crossDayProblem`** refuses any change that makes that day's first shift start before the overnight one has ended ("Mon 12 Oct's overnight shift runs until 06:00, after Tue 13 Oct's first shift starts at 05:00"). The check runs when a date is set or cleared (`setWorking`, `setDayOff`, `clearOverride`, `copyWeek`), for the weekly days and default shifts (the next four weeks), and across a rotation's whole cycle. A refused change is rolled back completely (`guarded`), and the dialog stays open with the reason; with several people ticked, the ones before the clash are already saved.

### Past midnight
A shift that runs past midnight belongs to the day it **starts**: Monday 22:00 to 06:00 is Monday's shift and Tuesday has its own schedule (the early hours of Tuesday are not a second day, see the clash rule above). So an overnight shift has to be the **last** shift of its day. Attendance keeps the check-out on `date` with `clockOutNextDay: true` (`workedMinutes` adds the day, the table shows `+1`, the sheet "next day", the export `06:00 (+1)`).

A day resolves to `{ working: true, shift: { start?, end?, flexible }, source }` or `{ working: false, reason, source }`. `scheduleLabel` renders it (`10:00 to 18:00`, `Flexible`, `Day off`, `Sick day`...).

## Pages and components
- `app/pages/schedule/index.vue`: three tabs, kept in `?tab=` (`week`, `shifts`, `defaults`; a link such as `/schedule?tab=defaults` opens one). **Week**: the grid (Monday to Sunday), staff rows, week navigation (only on this tab), search and role filter, an "Off each day" footer. A cell is a button: it shows the hours or the kind of day off, and a **Changed** or **Extra** marker when a dated override applies (the note shows in a tooltip). Active people only, the owner is left out.
- `schedule/ScheduleDayDialog.vue`: set a day (or a From to To range, at most 62 days) for **one person** (a cell) or **several** (tick rows, then **Assign**) as **Working day** (a shift, role hours or custom hours, note), **Day off** (type, note) or **Weekly default** (removes the override).
- `schedule/SchedulePersonSheet.vue`: the icon on a row (calendar-cog, or the repeat icon when the person has a rotation). **One sheet for one person's usual schedule**: it shows what the role gives them, then **The same every week** (day toggles, one or several shifts, "Back to the role") or **A rotation** (start date, 1 to 8 weeks, a Select per day, quick fills). Picking exactly the role's days with no shift stores nothing, so the person keeps following the role if it changes. Saving "the same every week" ends a rotation (it says so); saving a rotation keeps the weekly days they had. A single day is changed in the grid, not here.
- **Shifts tab** (`schedule/ScheduleShiftsPanel.vue`): list, create, edit and delete templates (a form beside the list, delete asks first). An empty list offers **Add common shifts** (the four demo ones).
- **Role defaults tab** (`schedule/ScheduleDefaultsPanel.vue`): one block per role (roles with people first): Flexible or Fixed hours, working days, and the shifts. Every change applies at once through `setRoleHours`; a refusal says why (toast) and leaves the role as it was. **This is the only place a role's working hours are edited**: `RoleDetailSheet` shows them read-only with an "Edit in Schedule" link.
- **Copy last week** (page toolbar) copies the dated changes of last week onto the shown week for the ticked people (everyone shown when none is ticked), replacing that week's own changes; the weekly pattern is shared, so only overrides move. Cells of a named shift take its tone and show its name; a legend lists the shifts.
- Menu: Operations, Members, after Attendance.

## State
`useSchedule()` (`useState('schedule-person-days')`, `'schedule-person-shifts'` (user id to a list of shift ids), `'schedule-shifts'`, `'schedule-rotations'`, `'schedule-overrides'`, `'schedule-restored'`): `scheduleFor(userId, date)`, `isWorkDay`, `workDaysFor(userId, range, today?)`, `plannedMinutes(userId, date)` (paid minutes of a **named** shift, else undefined), `weekDaysFor`, `overrideFor`, `overridesInRange`, `setDayOff`, `setWorking`, `clearOverride` (these and `setPersonDays`, `setPersonShifts` return a reason when refused), `setPersonDays`, `setPersonShifts`, `setRotation`, `removeRotation`, `carryOverFor`, `addShift`, `updateShift`, `removeShift`, `shiftUsage`, `copyWeek` (returns `{ count, problem? }`). Saved under `elev8-schedule` (version 4, guarded on `typeof localStorage`; older copies are read: version 1 gets the demo shifts, version 2 turns each default shift id into a one item list and `second` into `extra`, version 3 gets no rotations). Like `useActivityReport`, the saved copy is **read after mount**, not in the `useState` initialiser (Nuxt SSR does not re-run it); nothing is written until `restored` is true. A person whose role is unknown keeps the old rule (every day but Sunday). Mutations replace arrays and objects.

Seed (`buildSeedSchedule`, relative to today): the demo people on flexible roles (and `user-7`, a housekeeper on a seven day role) get **Monday to Saturday** as their own days, so Sunday is still off as before. Dated overrides: a sick day 5 days ago (`user-3`), a split shift 2 days ago (`user-3`, 07:00 to 11:00 and 16:00 to 20:00, so Attendance shows two records), two days of leave coming up (`user-2`) and two extra shifts (`user-7` Morning, `user-6` Afternoon). Nobody has a default shift in the seed.

## What reads it
- **Attendance** (`useAttendanceReport`): **a day has one record per shift.** `AttendanceRecord.segment` is `1` (or unset), `2`, `3`...; each later shift of a split-shift day is its own record (`att-<user>-<date>-<n>`) with its own check in, check out, photos, late and early flags. An absence is a **scheduled shift** (up to yesterday) with no record, so a day off is never absent and a missed second shift counts once (the card says Absences; without split shifts it is a count of days). The seed (`buildSeedAttendance(now, people, schedule?)`) only makes records on scheduled days, one per shift against that shift's hours; flexible days are never late or early; a second shift draws from its own generator so nobody else's values change. Without a `schedule` it keeps the old rules (Sundays off, the seed shifts), which the specs rely on. The table marks the second record with a **Shift 2** badge, and the detail sheet shows **Scheduled** for that record's shift ("Shift 2 of 2: 16:00 to 20:00"). The export gives a day with a split shift an extra "in" and "out" pair per extra shift (`<day>, shift 2`, `shift 3`), and "Total present" counts days.
- **Mobile contract**: the mobile app is not in this repo. Each clock-in must say which shift it is (`segment` 1, 2, 3 or 4) so the dashboard can match it to the day's schedule. A person on a split shift clocks in and out once per shift. A check-out after midnight is sent as the clock time with `clockOutNextDay: true`.
- **Activities** (`useActivityReport`, `/activities/[id]`): the target (per working day up to today: a named shift's paid minutes, else the person's `hoursPerDay`), "days not logged" and the daily chart's workday shading use `useSchedule`.

## The grid cell (`schedule/ScheduleCell.vue`) and Attendance
A cell **summarises**; the detail is in a hover card. Fixed height (`h-16`), so rows stay even. A **Schedule | Attendance** switch above the grid (remembered in `localStorage` under `elev8-schedule-view`, read after mount) picks what the cell is about.
- **Schedule view**: one chip per shift (a colour dot, the name, the hours short as `10-18`, `07:30-16:30`, `22-06 +1`; at most two, then "+N more"), "Flexible" muted for a role with no hours, "Day off" or the kind of leave dashed and muted. A footer row holds small markers on the left (moon: an overnight shift of the day before runs into this day; repeat: rotation; pencil: changed for this day) and, once the time has passed, **one status** on the right: the worst of the day's shifts (`dayStatus`: Absent, No check-out, Late, Left early, Working, On time; the first four in the destructive colour). Days that come from the role or the person's own schedule are plain; days that are changed or in a rotation get a primary border.
- **Attendance view**: one line per shift with what was clocked (`08:05-17:02`, `+1` past midnight, `now` while open today, `?` for no check-out on a past day), the problems in the destructive colour, "Absent" for a missed shift, `-` when there is nothing, "Day off" kept on a day off (with any record shown).
- **Hover card** (opens after 250 ms, also on focus): planned shifts, where the day comes from ("From the role", "Their own schedule", "Rotation", "Changed for this day") and its note, the overnight carry-over, what was clocked with its late or early notes, **Change this day** and **Open in Attendance** (sets Attendance's range to that day and goes there; it does not filter the person).
- The data is `useAttendanceReport().attendanceStatus(userId, date)`: one entry per shift that has a record or was missed, only up to today. Same rules as the Attendance page: nothing before a person's first record, **today is never absent**, the future shows nothing, a record on a day off is kept, each shift of a split-shift day on its own. Pure helpers in `app/lib/schedule-cell.ts` (`shortRange`, `dayStatus`, `clockedLabel`), spec `tests/lib/schedule-cell.spec.ts`. The cell's screen-reader label has the plan and what was clocked. The record itself is read-only here (see `attendance.md`).

## Undo and messages
- **Every change goes through `useScheduleUndo().run(message, change)`** (`app/composables/useScheduleUndo.ts`): the day dialog, the person sheet, the Role defaults tab, the Shifts tab and Copy last week. It takes a snapshot first (`useSchedule().snapshot()`: overrides, weekly days, default shifts, rotations, the shift list and every role's working hours). `change` returns a reason when it is refused: then **everything it did is put back** (a change to several people is all or nothing), the reason is shown with "Nothing was saved." and nothing else happens. Otherwise a toast says what was done with an **Undo** (8 seconds). Undo restores the snapshot (`restoreSnapshot`), unless another change was made since (`changeId` counter), in which case it says so instead of overwriting that change. `message` can be a function, for a count found by the change.
- **Messages say how to fix it**: they keep the plain statement first and add the fix ("Shift 2 has to start after shift 1 ends (11:00). It starts at 10:00, so start it at 11:00 or later.", "...Start Tue 13 Oct's shift at 06:00 or later, or end the overnight shift earlier."). Specs match on the first part. Wording in the UI avoids the code words: it says "Changed" for a day set on a date (not override), "the usual schedule" for what a person follows without a change, "Weeks before it repeats" for a rotation, and "Used by 2 roles, 3 people, 1 rotation, 4 single days" on a shift.
- ⚠️ Toggle groups: shadcn `ToggleGroupItem` is `flex-1` with a zero basis, so inside a `w-fit` group the items collapse and clip their labels. Give the group `w-full` (the person sheet) or the items `flex-none px-4` (Role defaults).

## Rules and gotchas
- **Roles pick shifts** (`users/RoleDetailSheet.vue`, `WorkingHours.shiftIds`): a fixed role's hours are shifts from the same shift list as everyone else, one or several (a split day). `startTime` and `endTime` are kept as the first start and last end for anything that still reads plain times (the mobile app may), and they are the fallback when a role has no shift, or its shift was deleted. Working hours apply to everyone in the role straight away, not only to new users (permissions and notifications are still defaults for new users). `setRoleHours(roleId, hours)` refuses no working day, shifts that overlap or no longer exist, and an overnight shift that runs into the first shift of the next working day, since every working day has the same shifts (`workingHoursProblem(hours, byId)`). Switching a role to Fixed starts it on the Office shift (else the first). It sorts the shifts by start and rewrites the plain times (`hoursFromShifts`). The Roles page sheet only summarises the hours and counts people. `RolesGrid` shows the shift names (`Morning + Night`).
- **Conversion of old roles** (`convertRoleHours`, run once on restore): a fixed role saved before this that only has plain times is pointed at the shift with the **same start and end** (any break), or a new shift named `10:00 to 18:00` is created from them (no break, so the paid time stays the same). The demo roles already carry `shift-office` (10:00 to 18:00, no break) and `shift-day` (08:00 to 17:00, 1 hour break, 8 paid hours). The hours people work do not change. ⚠️ A role's own shift is a **named** shift, so its paid minutes now set the Activities target (an 8 hour shift gives the same as `hoursPerDay` 8, a shift with a break does not) and grid cells of role days take the shift's colour.
- **Repair on load** (first step of `convertRoleHours`): a role whose `shiftIds` name a shift the saved list does not have (a list saved before the demo `shift-office` existed) gets the demo shift added back, and an id nobody knows is dropped, after which the role is converted from its plain times. Without this the role's Select on the Role defaults tab is blank.
- Deleting a shift (`removeShift`) takes it out of every role that used it; a role left with none keeps its plain times. The Shifts sheet counts roles, people, rotations and days per shift.
- ⚠️ Roles are the default, so editing a role's working days changes everyone without their own weekly days. The seeded demo days (above) are stored on the person, so they do not follow a role edit until reset with the weekly dialog.
- ⚠️ The seed attendance is a **history made once** per load from the schedule at that time. A day off added later does not rewrite a record that already exists; it only stops a missing record from counting as absent.
- ⚠️ The seed activity logs (`activities/data/activities.ts`) still skip only Sundays, so a Monday to Friday person can have logs on a Saturday. Nothing flags that.
- Dates are local `YYYY-MM-DD` keys (`toLocalDateKey`), never `toISOString()`.
- Hours use `base/TimePicker.vue`, never a native time input.
- Not built yet: more than four shifts a day, copying a rotation to other people, several shifts per day in the rotation editor, staff day-off requests and approval, conflict warnings (everyone off the same day), Attendance status "worked on a day off", a profile Schedule tab, the permission gate (no module in the role matrix yet).
- ⚠️ The seed attendance closes yesterday's overnight shift even when it would still be running this morning (the data is made once per load), and `toClock` now wraps past midnight instead of clamping at 23:59.
- ⚠️ The demo overrides are relative to today, so specs that use fixed dates pick a user with none (`user-8`) or clear `overrides` first.
- Tests: `tests/lib/schedule.spec.ts`, `tests/composables/useSchedule.spec.ts`.
