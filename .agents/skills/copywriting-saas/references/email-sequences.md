# SaaS Lifecycle Email Sequences

Email is the primary bridge between a visitor signing up for a SaaS product and becoming a long-term, high-LTV customer. Effective SaaS emails focus on driving user behavior toward the **"Aha!" moment**.

---

## 1. The Welcome & Quick-Win Email (Day 0, Instant)

**Goal**: Deliver the first taste of value within 10 minutes of signup. Avoid corporate fluff or introducing the CEO.

### Structure:
- **Subject**: Welcome to [Product] + [First immediate action]
  - *Example*: "Welcome to Elev8! Connect your first villa in under 2 minutes"
- **Opening**: Acknowledge the goal they want to achieve.
- **Single Clear Step**: One high-contrast button to reach the product activation milestone.
- **Support Reassurance**: Direct email reply goes to a real human.

### Sample Copy:
```text
Subject: Welcome to Elev8 (and goodbye to double-bookings)

Hi {{first_name}},

Welcome aboard! You’re joining 150+ villa hosts across Bali who use Elev8 to run their operations on autopilot.

Your account is set up and ready to go. The very first step is connecting your calendar so Elev8 can begin syncing your reservations across Airbnb and Booking.com:

👉 [Connect Your First Listing (Takes 2 min)]

Got a question or want help importing your existing bookings? Just hit reply to this email—our Bali support team is here to assist.

Best,
Komang & The Elev8 Team
```

## 2. The Feature Spotlight Email (Day 2–3)

**Goal**: Introduce ONE high-leverage feature that solves a known daily headache.

### Structure:
- **Subject**: How [Customer/Peer] does [Desired Outcome] in [Short Time]
  - *Example*: "How Uluwatu Cliff Villa saves 10 hours a week on guest messaging"
- **Story Hook**: Briefly describe the painful old way vs the automated new way.
- **Feature Walkthrough**: 2 bullet points on how to turn it on.
- **CTA**: Direct link to the relevant feature settings page.

### Sample Copy:
```text
Subject: Never answer "What's the WiFi password?" again

Hi {{first_name}},

Late-night guest messages about pool temperatures, checkout times, and WiFi codes are the fastest way to burn out as a host.

That’s why we built ElevAI into your unified inbox.

With automated journeys, ElevAI detects common guest questions on WhatsApp and drafts polite, accurate answers in seconds—saving you hours of typing every week.

Here’s how to enable it:
1. Open your Inbox tab
2. Click "ElevAI Settings"
3. Toggle on "Auto-response for common inquiries"

👉 [Turn On Auto-Responses in Elev8]

Give it a spin today and enjoy uninterrupted evenings!
```

## 3. The Trial Ending Countdown (Day 11 & Day 13 of a 14-day trial)

**Goal**: Remind the user with zero pressure, highlight what they'll lose access to, and invite an upgrade or extension.

### Email A: 3 Days Left (Value Recap)
```text
Subject: 3 days left on your Elev8 trial — here’s what you’ve accomplished

Hi {{first_name}},

Your 14-day free trial of Elev8 wraps up on Friday. 

Over the past two weeks, your account has:
• Synced {{sync_count}} reservations across your channels
• Automated {{message_count}} guest messages
• Saved an estimated {{hours_saved}} hours of manual data entry

To keep your calendars connected and your automated guest journeys active without interruption, pick a plan that fits your portfolio:

👉 [Select Your Elev8 Plan]

Need a few more days to test things with your housekeeping team? Just reply and let me know—happy to extend your trial by a week.
```

### Email B: 24 Hours Left (Urgency & Protection)
```text
Subject: Quick heads up: Your Elev8 trial ends tomorrow

Hi {{first_name}},

Your free trial expires in 24 hours. 

To make sure your calendar sync and WhatsApp auto-replies don’t pause, take 60 seconds to activate your subscription:

👉 [Keep Elev8 Active]

If you're not ready right now, no hard feelings! Your settings and listings will remain safely saved in case you decide to return later.

Cheers,
The Elev8 Team
```

## 4. Churn Prevention & Cancellation Feedback

**Goal**: If an active user requests cancellation or downgrades, listen with empathy and remove friction.

```text
Subject: We’re sorry to see you go, {{first_name}}

Hi {{first_name}},

Your Elev8 subscription has been cancelled and you won’t be charged again. Your access will remain active until the end of your billing cycle on {{expiry_date}}.

We know software changes can be frustrating, and we’re constantly working to improve Elev8. If you have 30 seconds, could you tell us what we could have done better?

[ ] Missing a feature my team needed
[ ] Found a tool that fits my workflow better
[ ] Price didn't match our current volume
[ ] Too complicated to set up / train staff

Either way, thank you for giving us a shot. If your needs change down the road, your listings will be right here waiting for you.
```
