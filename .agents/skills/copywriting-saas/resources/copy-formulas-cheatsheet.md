# SaaS Copywriting Formulas & Swipe Cheatsheet

Quick-reference formulas for high-impact headlines, section copy, and call-to-action buttons.

---

## 1. Core Persuasion Formulas

### PAS (Problem — Agitate — Solve)
*Best for*: Hero subheadlines, problem sections, sales emails.
- **Problem**: State the acute pain point directly.
- **Agitate**: Turn the knife. Paint the emotional and financial cost of ignoring the problem.
- **Solve**: Introduce your product as the clear, relief-giving solution.
> *Example*:  
> **Problem**: Villa hosts lose up to 15 hours a week manually answering guest inquiries across WhatsApp and Airbnb.  
> **Agitate**: In peak season, one slow reply means a lost direct booking worth $1,500—or worse, a 3-star review for poor communication.  
> **Solve**: Elev8 unifies all your messaging channels and uses AI to reply accurately in under 10 seconds.

### BAB (Before — After — Bridge)
*Best for*: Case studies, testimonials, product tours.
- **Before**: Describe the chaotic current world.
- **After**: Describe the calm, profitable future world.
- **Bridge**: Show how your SaaS takes them from Before to After.
> *Example*:  
> **Before**: Juggling 4 spreadsheets, sticky notes on the fridge, and late cleanings.  
> **After**: Every housekeeping job automatically assigned to staff phones the moment a guest checks out.  
> **Bridge**: Elev8's Operations Calendar syncs real-time bookings with maintenance tasks in one place.

### FAB (Feature — Advantage — Benefit)
*Best for*: Feature cards, pricing tables, product updates.
- **Feature**: What it is (*"Two-way 3CX VoIP integration"*).
- **Advantage**: What it does (*"Automatically records and transcribes inbound phone calls into the guest thread"*).
- **Benefit**: Why the customer cares (*"Never forget a special dietary request or late airport pickup again"*).

### The 4 Us (Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-Specific)
*Best for*: Email subject lines, announcement banners, hero headlines.
- **Useful**: Does it promise a tangible benefit?
- **Urgent**: Is there a compelling reason to act now?
- **Unique**: Does it sound distinctly different from every other SaaS tool?
- **Ultra-specific**: Does it mention specific quantities, times, or metrics?

## 2. SaaS Headline Swipe Library

### A. The "Without" Formula
- `[Get Desired Outcome] without [Annoying Grind]`
- *"Boost direct villa reservations by 30% without paying hefty OTA commission fees."*
- *"Track housekeeping and maintenance without checking 5 different WhatsApp groups."*

### B. The "Built For" Formula
- `The [Category] purpose-built for [Hyper-targeted ICP]`
- *"The all-in-one hospitality operating system purpose-built for luxury Bali villas."*
- *"The payment request engine designed specifically for short-term rental hosts."*

### C. The Metric Transformation
- `From [Current Painful Metric] to [Desired Target Metric]`
- *"From 45-minute response times to instant 10-second guest satisfaction."*
- *"Cut turnover coordination time from 3 hours a day to 5 minutes."*

### D. The Stop/Start Contrast
- `Stop [Tiring Manual Activity]. Start [Automated Scaling].`
- *"Stop updating rate plans manually. Start maximizing RevPAR with smart dynamic rules."*
- *"Stop losing track of physical villa keys. Start real-time digital custody tracking."*

## 4. Power Verbs for SaaS Copy

Swap weak verbs (*optimize, leverage, facilitate, streamline, enhance*) for active, tangible verbs:

- **Speed / Efficiency**: *Automate, Accelerate, Reclaim, Fast-track, Eliminate, Bypass, Slash.*
- **Growth / Profit**: *Maximize, Boost, Capture, Skyrocket, Scale, Expand, Monetize.*
- **Control / Clarity**: *Track, Unify, Master, Safeguard, Monitor, Centralize, Oversee.*
- **Creation / Launch**: *Build, Deploy, Publish, Launch, Connect, Generate.*
