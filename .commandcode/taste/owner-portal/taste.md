- Owner portal charts layout: revenue-trend chart spans full width at top; remaining metrics (ADR, occupancy, etc.) split into half-width cards beneath. ADR is always its own standalone KPI card, never bundled into another chart. Confidence: 0.85
- Contract e-sign must capture a DRAWN signature on a canvas signature pad (mouse + touch, with Clear and a "Signature captured" state), not a click-to-sign button — the user requested "sign nya pakai draw". - Owner contract operational costs are modeled as a PERCENTAGE (0-100%) of operational costs borne by the owner — 100% = owner covers all costs, 0% = management company absorbs them — never as a fixed currency amount or per-occupied-night rate. The user explicitly rejected the previous fixed/occupancy amount model in favor of percentage ("pakai percentage misal 100% artinya semua cost ditanggung owner"). Confidence: 0.855
- Operational cost share is configured at OWNER CREATION (onboarding/Assignments step, per property), not on the Contract tab — the user redirected it there ("taruh di pada saat create owner jangan di contract"). The Contract tab shows it as read-only summary ("Set during owner creation"), and contract generation reads the stored percentage rather than an editable form on the detail sheet. Confidence: 0.85
- When the user changes a domain value's representation (e.g. operational fee from amount → percentage), update EVERY consuming surface in the same pass, including generated artifacts: owner contract page, admin detail sheet, the jsPDF document, and the Document Center document body ("update juga di pdf"). Confidence: 0.8
- Owner self-service actions complete immediately without staff approval: cancelling an owner stay must NOT require management approval ("seharusnya itu tidak butuh approval dari staff") — only a confirmation dialog ("Are you sure you want to cancel?" with Yes/No) gates the destructive action. The previous 72h management-approval window for cancellations was removed entirely (composable logic, dialog copy, alerts, and tests updated in one pass, with unused helpers kept backward-compatible). Confidence: 0.7
