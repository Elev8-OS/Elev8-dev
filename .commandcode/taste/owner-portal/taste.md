- The "Owner-use night cap" SETTING belongs in the owner's Self-booking tab (grouped with booking mode + seasonal quota windows, where the owner can both see and set their annual nights quota) — NOT in the Overview tab's owner-edit form or read-only summary. - Owner booking/quota settings configured on the detail sheet's Self-booking tab must ALSO be configurable at owner creation time — the user asked for quota settings when adding an owner ("tolong buat settingan quota itu juga pada saat add owner"), just as they earlier routed the operational cost share to the onboarding flow. When the user asked "kuota stay per yearnya 14 kenapa bisa add window melebihi kuota" (annual cap is 14 — why can I add a window exceeding it?), the fix added validation in `upsertQuota` rejecting any window with `maxNights` > the owner's `annualOwnerUseNightCap` (0/absent cap = unlimited), with unit tests covering the over-cap rejection and at-cap acceptance. Terse bug reports that expose a quota/window inconsistency are treated as a signal to enforce the constraint in the validation layer, not just surface a warning. Confidence: 0.6
